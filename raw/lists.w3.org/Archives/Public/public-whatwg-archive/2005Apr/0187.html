The element SMALL should be deprecated, as it describes the appearance 
of the content.
Alternatively, a new name for the element could be created.

Since SMALL is usually used to describe copyright-notices and other 
legal descriptions,
my proposal for a new name is DISCLAIMER or NOTES.
