> Advocates of the feature now have up to 3 months to present either a thorough test suite or a reasonably complete implementation for hgroup. That would be until March 12, 2013.

No one presented a test suite or implement on hgroup by the Mar 12 deadline.

*** Decision of the Working Group ***

Therefore, the HTML Working Group hereby endorses the request to remove hgroup from the HTML 5.0 CR specification.  The Editors are requested to make this change.

The WG Chairs have also agreed to leave the decision on how to handle hgroup in HTML 5.1 to the Editors.
